Research

Restricted Stenger Conjecture

2025

I proved the key lemma of the restricted Stenger conjecture, which describes the positive stability of specially defined matrices. By linking eigenvalues to linear ordinary differential equations and using rigorous analytical methods, I derived the characteristic polynomial of eigenvalues and determined the valid index range of orthogonal polynomials that validate the conjecture.
